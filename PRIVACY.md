# Privacy policy

Music Filter, last updated 2026-10-27.

**Music Filter collects no data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server.

## What it does with audio

- It runs only on `youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`.
- When you turn the filter on, it reads the audio that YouTube already streams to your browser and filters out the music on your device.
- That audio stays in memory, in the tab, for a few minutes at most. It is discarded when you turn the filter off, leave the page or close the tab.
- Nothing is written to disk and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Network access

The extension makes no network requests of its own. The filtering model and all code ship inside the extension.

## Settings

The on/off state lives only in the open tab. Nothing is stored between visits.
