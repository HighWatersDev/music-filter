Music Filter removes background music from YouTube videos in Safari and keeps the voices.

To turn it on, open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Music Filter, switch it on and allow it on youtube.com.

Then play a video on youtube.com in Safari and tap “Music filter: OFF” at the top left.
